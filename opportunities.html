Prospective Researchers & Students

PhD & Research Opportunities

We seek dedicated, inquisitive scholars interested in the molecular design of functional probes, interfacial electrochemistry, and sensing technology. Read below for details on how to join our laboratory at NIT Calicut.

Important Note on Admissions: Formal admission to the PhD program is governed exclusively by NIT Calicut's centralized institutional admission procedures (conducted bi-annually, typically Monsoon and Winter sessions). Prospective students holding independent national fellowships (CSIR/UGC-JRF, INSPIRE, etc.) are strongly encouraged to contact Dr. Ranjana before or during the application cycle.

1. Doctoral (Ph.D.) Research Positions

Regular Full-Time / Fellowship-Supported Scholars

Target Academic Background

M.Sc. or equivalent in General Chemistry, Organic Chemistry, Analytical Chemistry, Applied Chemistry, Materials Science, or Allied Chemical Sciences with strong academic standing.

Preferred Preparation & Skills

  • Sound conceptual understanding of organic reaction mechanisms and molecular structure.
  • Prior exposure to basic spectroscopy (UV-Vis, Fluorescence, NMR) or electrochemistry (CV, EIS).
  • Scientific curiosity, high work ethic, and good academic writing abilities.

Primary Activities in Group

Doctoral candidates will design and synthesize functional organic sensors, perform spectral titration kinetics, modify electrode surfaces, analyze complex analytical datasets, and prepare research manuscripts and patents.

Funding Paths

National Fellowships (CSIR-JRF / UGC-JRF / INSPIRE), NITC Institute Fellowships, or sponsored research assistantships as available.

2. Research Internships (M.Sc. & B.Sc. / BS-MS Students)

Summer Internships & Master's Thesis Projects (3 to 6 Months)

Eligibility & Placement

Motivated students pursuing B.Sc./BS-MS (final years) or M.Sc. in Chemistry/Chemical Sciences seeking short-term training, summer research fellowships (e.g., INSA-IASc-NASI), or completion of their final-semester master's dissertation.

Scope of Work

Hands-on learning of laboratory synthesis, spectroscopic titration methods, dye purification, literature reviews, or data plotting using software such as Origin and ChemDraw.

3. Project-Funded Research Positions (JRF / Project Associate)

Sponsored Extramural Projects (SERB / Anusandhan NRF / DST / CSIR)

When extramural research grants are awarded, project vacancies (such as Junior Research Fellow or Project Associate I/II) will be officially advertised on the NIT Calicut website careers page and notified here. Candidates with valid GATE or NET qualifications are typically preferred according to funding agency guidelines.

4. Interdisciplinary Research Collaborations

Academic & Institutional Partnerships

We actively welcome collaborative opportunities with researchers across microbiology, biomedical engineering, materials science, and device physics to test our synthesized probes in living models, fabricate flexible micro-electrodes, or pursue joint grant applications.

How to Apply / Express Interest

Prospective candidates wishing to discuss research opportunities should send an email inquiry directly to Dr. Ranjana with the following materials:

1. Comprehensive CV Detailed academic records, marks/grades, competitive exam scores (GATE, CSIR-NET, etc.), and prior project experience.
2. Statement of Interest A 1-page write-up describing which specific research themes in our group interest you and your academic career objectives.
3. Academic Transcripts Consolidated or provisional grade sheets for B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees.
4. Fellowship Proof Award letter or scorecard of CSIR/UGC-NET JRF, INSPIRE, or GATE (if qualified).
Email Subject Format: Prospective PhD / Intern Inquiry - [Your Name] - [Fellowship Status]
