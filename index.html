About

I am a chemical scientist with expertise spanning functional organic materials, molecular probe design, fluorescence diagnostics, electrochemical sensing, and nanomaterials. My research focuses on designing and synthesizing responsive functional molecules, novel fluorophores, and composite catalytic interfaces that address critical challenges at the intersection of chemistry, biology, and materials science.

I earned my Ph.D. in Chemical Sciences from Manipal Institute of Technology, Manipal Academy of Higher Education (MAHE) with distinction (CGPA: 8.83). My doctoral research centered on the design, photophysical investigation, and electrochemical characterization of nitronaphthalimide-based multifunctional probes for the optical and electrochemical detection of hydrogen sulfide (H₂S) and cellular imaging, providing a strong foundation in synthetic organic methodology, molecular recognition, redox behavior, and advanced analytical characterization. Prior to this, I completed my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Chemistry at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, receiving the University Gold Medal for postgraduate academic excellence.

To expand my research repertoire and collaborate across international benchmarks, I served as a Visiting Research Scholar at Aalto University, Espoo, Finland, conducting advanced nanoscale materials characterization, surface analysis, and spectroscopic evaluations. I also gained valuable foundational research experience at premier national laboratories in India: CSIR–Central Salt and Marine Chemicals Research Institute (CSMCRI), Gujarat, focusing on catalytic hydroformylation of olefins, and the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Thiruvananthapuram (IISER TVM), investigating active catalytic sites for 4-electron electrocatalytic oxygen reduction reactions (ORR).

Currently, I serve as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Education at the National Institute of Technology Calicut (NITC), Kerala, India. My research interests encompass synthetic organic probes, flexible and wearable electrochemical sensors, nanocarbon composite interfaces, and optical diagnostic platforms. I am passionate about mentoring motivated scholars and translating fundamental molecular-level sensing principles into accessible, miniaturized, and cost-effective analytical devices with real-world applications in healthcare diagnostics, bioimaging, and environmental surveillance.

Molecular Probes & Optical Diagnostics

Multi-stimuli-responsive fluorophores (naphthalimide derivatives, hybrid scaffolds) for real-time cellular imaging of gasotransmitters (H₂S) and selective fungal pathogen detection[cite: 3, 4].
