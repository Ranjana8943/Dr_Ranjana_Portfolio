Key Innovations

Core Research Thrusts

Bridging chemical synthesis, surface engineering, and biomedical diagnostics.

Functional Organic Probes

Engineering novel nitronaphthalimide fluorophores and electrochemical markers tailored for specific detection of endogenous gasotransmitters (H₂S) and bio-pathogen tracking.

Wearable Interfaces

Architecting molecular-nanocarbon composites on flexible matrices, elevating electron transfer kinetics to build ultra-responsive biosensors.

Point-of-Care Diagnostics

Translating complex laboratory testing protocols into cost-effective, portable sensory systems suitable for clinical monitoring and environmental testing.
