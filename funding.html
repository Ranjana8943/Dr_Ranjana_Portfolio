Financial Support Pathways in India

PhD Funding & Fellowship Guide

Doctoral research in science disciplines at premier institutes like NIT Calicut is supported through several established national and institutional funding routes. This guide outlines the key mechanisms available to eligible chemistry scholars.

Please Note: Holding a national fellowship confirms your financial award but does not bypass institutional PhD admission. All candidates must formally apply and qualify through the official NIT Calicut admission evaluation. Fellowship award norms, tenures, and allowances are governed solely by the respective funding agencies.

Which Funding Route Should I Explore?

Scenario A: You hold CSIR/UGC-JRF or DST-INSPIRE

You are eligible to apply under the direct fellowship category at NIT Calicut. Please write to Dr. Ranjana in advance to discuss research alignment.

Scenario B: You have a valid GATE or NET-LS score

You can apply for regular full-time PhD admission under Institute Fellowship (MoE funding), subject to seat availability, department criteria, and interview performance.

Scenario C: Post-M.Sc. without National Exam

Consider qualifying for national exams (GATE / CSIR-NET) or apply for Project Associate positions on externally funded grants as and when advertised.

1. CSIR-UGC NET Junior Research Fellowship (JRF)

CSIR-HRDG Portal

The premier national competitive exam for chemical sciences. Qualified candidates receive independent fellowship support directly from CSIR or UGC for up to 5 years (subject to satisfactory progress).

Eligibility: M.Sc. in Chemical Sciences or allied areas with minimum 55% marks (or as specified by CSIR-UGC notification).

Admission Link: Allows candidates to compete for PhD admission at NIT Calicut under the sponsored/external fellowship category.

2. DST INSPIRE Fellowship

DST INSPIRE Portal

A flagship scheme by the Department of Science and Technology (DST) for university rank holders (1st rank in post-graduation in basic science) and INSPIRE Scholars with high CGPA.

Eligibility: University Gold Medalists / 1st Rank Holders in M.Sc. Chemistry or eligible INSPIRE scholars.

Process: Requires provisional admission into a Ph.D. program before final fellowship sanction by DST.

3. NIT Calicut Institute Research Fellowship

NITC Admissions Portal

Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India fellowship granted directly through NIT Calicut institutional admission rounds for full-time regular research scholars.

Eligibility: Master’s degree in relevant discipline with a valid GATE score or qualifying rank in national-level examinations as prescribed in the current NITC PhD admission bulletin.

Selection: Written test and interview conducted by the institutional selection committee.

4. Externally Sponsored Project Fellowships (JRF / PA)

As Available Upon Project Sanction

Tenured research staff appointments funded by national funding agencies (e.g., SERB, DST, CSIR, DBT). Project staff meeting institutional requirements may also have opportunities to apply for regular Ph.D. registration as per NITC ordinances.
